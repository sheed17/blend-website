Blend parcel assets

Flat parcels for the hero beam, a rotating 3D box built in pure CSS, and a live demo of parcels flowing into Blend.

1. Beam parcels (SVG)

Small isometric boxes for the hero beam. The dented one carries a crack and a crushed corner.

Copy these SVGs straight into the hero. They stay crisp at 14px.

2. Rotating 3D parcel (pure CSS)

No model file, no library. Hover to pause. Could sit in the hero or above the closing line.

3. Live beam demo

Parcels travel in along five beams, some arrive damaged, Blend pulses, and recovered $ flows out. This is roughly what the hero upgrade would do.
